Inside the train, the milk was reading a newspaper. It warned Santa that the next stop was yesterday. Santa wanted to collect a forgotten present, so he stayed in his seat. The train rattled through a tunnel made of cold carrots and stopped beside his own kitchen table.

Yesterday's Santa was still looking for the butter. Today's Santa handed it over and collected the present. Then both Santas reached for the last biscuit. The mouse blew his whistle. Only one passenger could return, so they settled the argument by sending the biscuit home first.
