A patient robot examined a small engine in the workshop. A wheel was loose, so it tightened the bolt and tested the machine again. The engine ran smoothly. The robot put its tools away and wrote a short note about the repair.

The peaceful forest stretched across the valley. Tall trees sheltered the path from the warm sun. A stream passed between the roots and flowed toward a lake. Two travelers followed the path until they reached a wooden bridge above the water.

A playful cat rested beside the window. She opened her eyes when a bird landed in the garden. After watching it for a while, she stretched and walked to her bowl. The house was quiet, and she returned to her basket after eating.

A bright planet appeared above the mountains. Its light was reflected from a nearby star. The astronomer compared its position with a drawing from the previous week. She wrote the new observation in her notebook and adjusted the telescope for another look.

Several robots repaired the wooden gate together. One held the hinge in place while the others tightened the screws. When the work was complete, the gate opened easily. The gardener thanked them and walked along the path to inspect the young trees.

The river carried fallen leaves past the village. A child watched from the bridge and followed one leaf with her eyes. It turned around a stone, moved beneath a branch, and disappeared beyond the bend. The child returned home as the evening became cool.
