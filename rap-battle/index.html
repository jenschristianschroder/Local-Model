    How the models work

    A shared reply model reads the previous challenge, who said it, and the word you pick. A challenge model writes the final two lines in the current opponent’s voice. Octopus uses a dedicated challenge model. All generation runs in this browser. When a reply model emits its learned copy token, your word appears in the reply. Each turn gets a fresh random seed. If a sample repeats a line or pair of ending words, the browser can retry at a slightly higher temperature.
